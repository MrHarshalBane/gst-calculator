GST 2.0 Reform Guide & FAQs

Everything you need to know about Goods & Services Tax in India for 2026 and beyond

What are the GST tax slabs in 2026+?

Under India's streamlined GST framework, tax rates are consolidated into core categories: 0% (Nil) for essentials & insurance, 3% for gold & jewellery, 5% for mass consumption items, 18% standard rate for most electronics & services, and 40% for luxury/sin goods (tobacco, >350cc bikes, aerated drinks).

Difference between CGST, SGST, and IGST?

For Intra-State sales (within the same state), GST is divided equally into CGST (Central Govt) and SGST (State Govt). For Inter-State transactions (across states), IGST (Integrated GST) applies at the full rate.

Exclusive vs Inclusive GST: How are they calculated?

• Exclusive (+ GST): Tax is added on top of the base amount. Tax = Price × Rate / 100.
• Inclusive (- GST): Tax is extracted from total price. Base = Total / (1 + Rate / 100).
